Our story

From good makers to UK shelves

We look for food and drink makers we admire in the USA, India, Taiwan and here in the UK, and help them reach UK shoppers. That means everything from honey-sweetened drinks made in New Jersey to iced chai canned in Britain, basmati rice from Rajasthan and teas from Sri Lanka.

We take care of the hard parts: importing, UK labelling, food safety checks and getting products to shops of every size, from the corner shop to the superstore.
