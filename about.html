Our story

Crafted in New Jersey

Bee’s Water is made by Bee’s Beverage Corporation in Hamilton, New Jersey. Each recipe pairs filtered water with organic honey, added vitamin C and organic plant extracts, for a drink that is lightly sweet, full of flavour and free from refined cane sugar and artificial sweeteners.
