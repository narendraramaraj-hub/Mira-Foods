Why honey?

Sweetness the way nature makes it

Instead of refined cane sugar or sweeteners, every bottle is sweetened only with organic honey. You get a soft, rounded sweetness and a taste you’ll recognise from the very first sip.

Each 473 ml bottle has 90 calories and 23 g of sugars, all of it from honey. We think you should know exactly what’s in your drink.
