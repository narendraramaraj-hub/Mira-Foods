Ingredients & wellness

What’s inside, and what isn’t

We keep it short and honest. Here’s every ingredient, why it’s there, and the full nutrition facts.

01

Filtered water

The base of every bottle: clean, filtered water.

02

Organic honey

Our only sweetener. It gives Bee’s Water its soft, rounded taste, without refined cane sugar, stevia or artificial sweeteners. TODO: confirm organic certification is recognised in Great Britain (UK–US organic equivalence) and add certifier code if required

03

Vitamin C

Added as ascorbic acid. Each 473 ml bottle contains 180 mg, which is 225% of the Nutrient Reference Value.

04

Plant extracts

Organic elderberry extract in Native Blueberry, and organic turmeric extract in Golden Ginger Lemon and Tangy Lemon, for flavour and character.

Every flavour also contains organic flavouring, citric acid and fruit or vegetable juice for colour. Full ingredient lists are on the flavours page.
