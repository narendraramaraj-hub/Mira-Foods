How to brew

Hot or iced

Hot

One teaspoon per cup. Black tea with freshly boiled water for 3 to 5 minutes; green and moringa tea a little cooler, for 2 to 3 minutes.

🧊

Iced

Brew double strength, cool, then pour over plenty of ice with a slice of lemon.
